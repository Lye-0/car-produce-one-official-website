## 車両・都市の参照
提供動画1〜4を都市の雰囲気・道路・広告・建物の参考、動画5を黒いヴェゼル、動画6をプレリュードの形状参考として使用。プレリュードの完成色はユーザー指定の暗い青。

車両の面・部品は今回制作した近似形状で、メーカーの3Dデータを取り出したものではない。既製の車両モデルの購入・ダウンロードは行っていない。

## 大型広告のオリジナル素材
組み込みの画像生成ツールを使用。生成画像は全てプロジェクト内に保存した。ブランドの実在する広告の転載ではない。

### atlas 1
保存先: `assets/textures/v15/urban-advertising-atlas.png`

生成プロンプト:
> Create one production texture atlas for large digital billboards in a realistic near-future Japanese commercial city at night. One image arranged as a precise 2 by 2 grid of four equal rectangular advertising artworks, no gutters or borders, front-facing flat artwork only, no billboards or buildings or frames shown. Top left: premium macro photographic portrait of a beautiful metallic blue and silver automotive wheel with turbine spokes on deep midnight blue, crisp technical luxury advertising art, tiny simple text 'MOTION'. Top right: photoreal editorial fashion portrait of a Japanese adult woman in a sculptural white coat against dark cyan light, simple white text 'NIGHT / FORM'. Bottom left: a premium photoreal close-up of a clear glass fragrance bottle and water ripples lit warm amber, simple text 'AFTER RAIN'. Bottom right: luminous flowing translucent aqua ribbon forming an elegant three-dimensional torus on a deep dark blue background, tiny text 'NEXT'. Sophisticated high end advertising, carefully composed elegant photography and restrained typography, intense but controlled highlights, distinguishable dark areas to retain night mood. Full bleed each quadrant. High resolution 2048 by 2048 square. These are original fictional ad visuals for 3D scene textures.

### atlas 2
保存先: `assets/textures/v15/urban-advertising-atlas-2.png`

生成プロンプト:
> One production texture atlas of four original commercial billboard artworks for a realistic near-future city. Precise equal 2 by 2 grid, no gaps and no frame, only flat front-facing full-bleed advertising artwork, no pictured billboards or city. Each quadrant must be visually different. Top left: warm premium cafe advertisement, photoreal steaming espresso in a porcelain cup, dark coffee beans, rich caramel and cream tones, minimal elegant text 'COFFEE'. Top right: bold futuristic athletic shoe product photography, a sculptural orange and white sneaker floating against a clean ivory background, minimal text 'MOVE'. Bottom left: premium over-ear headphones in soft mint green, photoreal product photography, pale mint and charcoal composition, minimal text 'LISTEN'. Bottom right: contemporary art exhibition poster, a beautiful photographic translucent violet and magenta flower in moody purple light against near black, large elegant minimal text 'BLOOM'. Sophisticated believable real-world commercial art direction, distinct color palettes, carefully controlled highlights, precise clean typography, detailed photography. Square 2048x2048 high resolution. These are fictional advertisements used as textures in a 3D urban scene.

生成結果はいずれも1254×1254px。各4種類、合計8種類の広告素材。建物の大型広告とバス停の小型広告に使用し、縦横比に応じてUV範囲を調整した。
